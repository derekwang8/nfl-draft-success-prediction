# nfl-draft-success-prediction
DS 4021 Final Project

# Motivation Narrative
In American sports, one of the best ways to add talent is through the amateur draft, where professional teams select college and amateur players. Perhaps the sport that values this draft most is American football. In the modern NFL Draft, teams have several chances to select players who could significantly impact not just their team, but the league as a whole. As a result, teams need the right draft picks to select players with those capabilities. However, these players are not always taken early in the draft. This project focuses on the opportunity to find “hidden gems” later in the draft.

# Research Question 
How accurately can information available at draft time predict successful NFL players, particularly those selected in rounds 4-7?

# Dataset Description
The NFL Draft 1985–2015 dataset, published by Ulrik Thyge Pedersen and downloaded from Kaggle [1], contains 8,435 draft-selection records and 34 variables covering the years 1985–2015. The variables include player identifiers, draft year, round, overall pick, team, position, college, and age, alongside NFL performance measures such as games played, Pro Bowl selections, and Approximate Value. Among the 7,190 records with age available, the mean recorded age was 22.64 years (SD = 0.84), ranging from 20 to 29 years. Age was missing for 1,245 records (14.8%). These data support examining the relationship between draft position and subsequent NFL performance, particularly the success of players selected in later rounds.
